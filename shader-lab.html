Pulse Lab · Shader Lab

Custom finishes, compiled onto the mask.

Pick a design or write your own TSL, watch it compile onto the full PulseMask model live, then copy the finish spec code and attach it to any order on the pricing page. The Lab is free to use. Every design shown here is the exact source that runs — the source pane cannot diverge from the render.

Visual layer only

Designs recolour the shell's PBR materials in the viewer. They never move geometry, never restyle the seal bead, visor optics or indicator surfaces, and never change what the exporters write — STL, OBJ and GLB carry the base materials. And as everywhere on this site: these files are wearable-art props, not PPE, not a respirator, not a medical device.
