Read before ordering

PulseMask is a design study, sold as wearable art. These files produce a decorative prop / costume / maker piece. It is not a respirator, not PPE, and not a medical device — it is untested to NIOSH 42 CFR 84, EN 136/143 and ISO 16900, provides no respiratory protection, and must never be relied on as protective equipment. UV-C hardware is an eye and skin hazard; no electronics are included or supported. Every order confirms this in writing.

How ordering works

No cart. No tracking. Email.

01 · Order

Every order button opens a pre-addressed email draft with the tier, the price and the details I need already filled in. Add your answers and send it. For Photo-Fit, attach your portrait and your IPD in the same message. Nothing about you touches this page — there is no cart, no account, no analytics, no cookies and no backend here.

02 · Confirm & pay

You get a written confirmation of scope, price and turnaround, with an invoice and a payment link. Work starts when payment clears. If a Photo-Fit portrait is unusable — lighting, angle, glasses — you hear that straight away, and you get a full refund if it cannot be made to work.

03 · Delivery

Files arrive by email — STL in millimetres, ready to slice, plus the print guide, the assembly walkthrough and your PMK- unlock code for in-browser exports on the generator. Revision rounds per your tier. If generation fails on our side, full refund; digital files otherwise carry no returns, which is why revisions exist.

Fine print

License

Personal license on every STL tier: print as many as you like for yourself, gift prints freely, never redistribute or resell the files, never sell prints. Selling prints requires a commercial print license — flat 5× the list tier price (not the sale price), per design, forever. Email to arrange.

What you print is on you

Files are provided as-is for decorative use. You are responsible for material choice, print quality and how a finished piece is worn. Keep the visor aperture edges and any line-of-sight rules from the design intact if you light it — and never install a UV-C emitter. That constraint is not negotiable at any price.

Why these prices

Stock files are priced like a good prop STL, because that is what they are. Photo-Fit costs more because a human generates, checks and revises a mesh that only fits one face on Earth. Commissions are priced like the engineering work they contain.
